The weekly plan, clearly shared

One company workspace for planning stations, publishing the complete week, handling leave, and delivering payroll documents privately.

What it does

The app turns the familiar paper station grid into a shared Monday-to-Friday plan. Managers can place several workers at one station, move assignments, add times and notes, use main stations, publish the week, share it, and print it on A4 landscape paper.

Planning the week

  • Choose a worker and click a cell, or drag the worker into it.
  • Several workers can share a station, but one worker cannot be booked at two stations on the same date.
  • Fill main stations fills empty days and keeps every manual change.
  • Click an assignment to edit its worker, time, or handover note. Add a daily note for information that applies to everyone.
  • Publish week makes the complete plan visible to workers and sends an in-app notification. Publishing again creates an updated revision.
Vacation and sick leave are date based. Once the accountant approves a request, those dates are blocked in the planner and protected by the database. Other dates remain assignable.

Main stations and exceptions

A main station is the worker’s normal location. If Mathi normally works in BÜRO but is manually placed elsewhere on Wednesday, Wednesday stays at the manually chosen station. Filling main stations later does not overwrite it.

Leave and payroll documents

Workers request vacation, sick leave, appointments, or other absence from Leave & Payroll. They can attach PDF, JPG, or PNG evidence up to 10 MB. The accountant verifies attachments before approval, records overtime, and uploads private monthly payslips. Workers see only their own private records. The app stores payroll documents; it does not calculate salaries.

Closed days

Official Austrian public holidays appear automatically as blank closed dates. Owners and admins can also add Betriebsurlaub ranges. The underlying schedule remains stored and returns if a company closure is removed. Even a completely closed week can be published.

Share, print, export, and audit

  • Share uses the phone share sheet with a WhatsApp-compatible fallback.
  • Print produces a plain black-and-white A4 landscape grid.
  • Settings exports the selected week and worker list as CSV.
  • The owner can inspect protected change history and export one year of audit records.

Account management

The owner invites each person, chooses the role, and links the login to a worker record when needed. Access can be disabled or deleted, and the same email can later be invited again. A login account and a scheduled worker are separate records.
